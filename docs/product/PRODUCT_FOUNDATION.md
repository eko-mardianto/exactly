# Product Foundation

Product Foundation defines what Exactly is as a product.

It establishes the product's purpose, intended users, the problems it solves, and the boundaries that guide future product decisions.

This document serves as the primary reference for product strategy before feature design and implementation begin.

---

## Product Vision

Make confident decision-making accessible through understanding.

---

## Product Mission

Transform complex information into clear understanding that supports confident decisions.

---

## Target Users

The Target Users defined in this document differ from those in the Brand Foundation.

Brand Foundation identifies the primary audiences that Exactly seeks to serve as a brand.

Product Foundation identifies the primary users who will receive the greatest value from the product itself.

While both documents describe similar groups of people, they do so from different perspectives:

- Brand Foundation focuses on **who Exactly exists to serve**.
- Product Foundation focuses on **who the product is designed for and the value it delivers**.

---

Exactly is designed for people who make decisions based on information and want to understand that information more clearly before acting.

The product is intended for users who value reasoning over assumptions and seek confidence through understanding rather than certainty without evidence.

The primary target users are:

### 1. Beginner Decision Makers

People who are new to a domain and need guidance to understand information before making important decisions.

Examples include first-time investors, students, career starters, and individuals learning unfamiliar topics.

---

### 2. Time-Constrained Professionals

People who regularly make decisions but have limited time to gather, organize, and evaluate large amounts of information.

The product helps them understand essential insights more efficiently without sacrificing reasoning.

---

### 3. Experienced Decision Makers

People who already possess domain knowledge but value structured reasoning, evidence evaluation, and transparent explanations to strengthen their own judgment.

The product strengthens human judgment rather than replacing it.

---

## Core Problems

Core Problems define the fundamental challenges that Exactly is designed to solve.

These problems should remain stable over time, regardless of future features, technologies, or implementation details.

Features may evolve.

The underlying problems should not.

---

### Understanding Before Acting

People often make decisions before fully understanding the information available to them.

---

### Fragmented Information

Relevant information is frequently scattered, fragmented, or difficult to connect into a coherent picture.

---

### Opaque Reasoning

Many existing tools provide answers or conclusions without sufficiently explaining the reasoning behind them.

---

### Limited Time for Evaluation

People have limited time to evaluate evidence, compare perspectives, and verify the quality of information before making decisions.

---

### Confidence Without Understanding

Confidence is often based on assumptions, opinions, or emotions rather than clear understanding supported by evidence.

---

## Value Proposition

Value Proposition defines the unique value that Exactly delivers to its users.

It explains why users would choose Exactly over alternative ways of understanding information and making decisions.

Rather than promising certainty or better outcomes, Exactly promises a better decision-making process through clearer understanding.

---

Exactly delivers value by helping users:

- Understand complex information more clearly.
- Connect fragmented information into meaningful insights.
- Evaluate evidence before reaching conclusions.
- Strengthen confidence through understanding rather than assumptions.
- Make better-informed decisions without replacing human judgment.

---

Exactly does not promise perfect decisions.

It helps people build the understanding needed to make decisions with greater confidence, transparency, and reasoning.

---

## Product Scope

Product Scope defines the boundaries of what Exactly is intended to provide.

It ensures that future product decisions remain aligned with the product's purpose and prevents unnecessary expansion beyond its core mission.

Exactly is designed to:

- Help users understand complex information more clearly.
- Organize and connect relevant information into meaningful context.
- Support evidence-based reasoning and evaluation.
- Present transparent explanations rather than unexplained conclusions.
- Strengthen human judgment throughout the decision-making process.

The product focuses on improving how people understand information before making decisions, regardless of the specific domain in which those decisions are made.

---

Product Scope defines what the product exists to do, not how it will be implemented.

Specific features, interfaces, and technologies may evolve over time while remaining within these boundaries.

---

## Non-goals

Non-goals define what Exactly is intentionally not designed to do.

They establish clear boundaries for future product decisions and help prevent unnecessary feature expansion beyond the product's purpose.

Exactly is not intended to:

- Replace human judgment or personal responsibility.
- Guarantee correct decisions or successful outcomes.
- Eliminate uncertainty from the decision-making process.
- Persuade users toward predetermined conclusions.
- Encourage decisions without sufficient understanding or evidence.
- Prioritize speed over reasoning and understanding.
- Become an autonomous decision-maker on behalf of its users.

These non-goals ensure that Exactly remains focused on strengthening human understanding rather than replacing human thinking.
